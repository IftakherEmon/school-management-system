About Our School

Learn more about our rich history, facilities, co-curricular clubs, and educational vision.

School History

Founded in 1995, ABC High School started with only a single building and a handful of classrooms. Over the last 30 years, it has expanded to become one of the premier high schools in the region, serving 1,250+ students. Our graduates study in leading national and international universities.

Our Vision

To nurture creative minds, build integrity, and cultivate leadership skills in every student to prepare them for global opportunities.

Our Mission

We provide a structured learning ecosystem that blends classroom teaching with sports, debate, science fairs, and community volunteering.

Our Facilities & Activities
